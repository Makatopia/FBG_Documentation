# Figure Blockout Generator

Figure Blockout Generator (FBG) is a Blender add-on for building proportioned human blockout figures.

<!-- TODO: hero image - posed figure with landmarks visible, showing what FBG produces -->
<!-- ![FBG overview](assets/images/home-hero-overview.avif) -->

It lets you generate a figure, shape its proportions, pose it through dedicated FBG properties in the panel rather than a traditional viewport rig, and animate it directly in Blender. When you are ready, you can bake the result into standard Blender data for further work.
